# 🏰 Buggy Empire 1830—1950 A single-file, fully offline grand-strategy simulation. You take the helm of a nation and steer it through 120 years of modern history, from 1830 to 1950 — managing domestic affairs, diplomacy, military, economy and state institutions, where every decision can push history down a different path. ## 🌐 Play Online / 在线试玩 👉 **[Click here to Play (点击这里直接游玩)](https://QAQ271911.github.io/SVVIP/)** *(No download required. 无需下载。)* ## ✨ Game Content * **Timeline 1830–1950**: Events, historical figures, unit types and treaties all appear at their real dates. * **Pick your nation and difficulty**: Every nation on the map is playable. * **Two modes**: Story Mode concludes at 1950; Infinite Mode never ends. * **Interactive panels**: Domestic affairs, finance, national strategy, society, construction, institutions, national policy, military, territory, recruitment, diplomacy, hegemony, world dispatch, the imperial court, media, archive, codex and more. * **1,000+ historical events**: De-duplicated and gated by era. * **Great-power AI**: Two dozen-plus nations pursue their own agendas. * **Institutions you build yourself**: Promulgate a constitution, transition to a republic, establish a federation. ## ⚠️ Help Wanted / 紧急求援 (中文开发者请看这里) **这是一个由纯小白 + AI 辅助开发、跨越1830-1950年的宏大历史网页战略游戏。** 坦白说，这个项目目前处于“废墟重建期”。由于早期 AI 生成了大量的代码，导致系统中堆满了死代码（Dead Code）和逻辑缠绕（Logic Entanglement）。目前 `index.html` 能打开主界面，但许多高级系统（如列国AI、蝴蝶效应、世界结算）仍然跑不通。 **我懂历史设定，但代码逻辑已经超出了我个人的精力范围。求各位懂 JS/前端的佬捞一把！** **How to contribute:** 1. Fork this repository. 2. Fix broken JS files in the `src/` folder, or help clean up dead code in `src/legacy/`. 3. Double-click `一键构建.bat` to rebuild `index.html`. 4. Submit a Pull Request (PR).
